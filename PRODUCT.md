# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

GitHub visitors, fellow students, researchers, collaborators, and recruiters who want a quick, memorable view of wenb's technical direction and current work.

## Product Purpose

Present wenb as a curious student developer exploring machine learning, graph foundation models, and medical imaging. The profile should make it easy to understand the research interests, discover Visionary Grove, visit the interactive portfolio, and make contact.

## Brand Personality

Curious, gentle, quietly heroic. The experience should feel like entering a living research garden: welcoming enough to explore, structured enough to trust, and playful without becoming childish.

## Anti-references

No pixel art, direct imitation of Stardew Valley, dark fantasy, neon cyberpunk dashboards, cluttered badge walls, generic beige portfolio templates, or animation that competes with the content.

## Design Principles

1. Turn research into a journey: explain technical interests as places to explore, not a list of buzzwords.
2. Let one mascot carry the story: reuse the garden knight as an identity anchor instead of scattering decorative icons.
3. Reward curiosity: interactions should reveal meaning, project context, or personality.
4. Keep the path obvious: visitors should reach the featured project, email, or GitHub in one clear action.
5. Bilingual by design: Chinese and English should feel composed together rather than duplicated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, preserve keyboard navigation and visible focus, maintain readable contrast, provide meaningful alternative text, and fully respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
